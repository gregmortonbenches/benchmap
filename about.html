About Bench Map UK

Britain has lots of brilliant benches. Bench Map UK draws on public data to help you find the best bench near you. You can rate, log and favourite benches.

Over 157,000 benches from OpenStreetMap, enriched with memorial inscriptions from OpenBenches.
